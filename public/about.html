About REVA

Acoustic wall panels, from the team at EZLIFE.

REVA Acoustic is EZLIFE's dedicated wall panel collection — PET fibre panels cut into geometric patterns, built to soften a room acoustically while giving the wall a finished, tailored look.

What we make

One collection, built for real rooms

The A Series spans 23 panel designs — from bold diamond pinwheels to quiet quilted grids — so there's a pattern that fits a living room, studio, office or home theater. Every design is offered in the same 5 finishes, which keeps mixing and matching designs on one wall simple.
